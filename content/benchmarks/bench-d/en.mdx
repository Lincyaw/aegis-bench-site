## Setting<About text="The agent can only read the observations from the time of the fault, and must reconstruct how the fault spread from its source to its symptoms." />

<Flow>
  <Step title="The agent receives">Metrics, logs and traces from the fault window, and the service topology.</Step>
  <Step title="How it works">Read-only queries of the observations; it cannot reach or change the running system.</Step>
  <Step title="What it submits">The propagation chain: the affected services in order, from source to symptom.</Step>
</Flow>

<Sample task="task-052" title="订单查询延迟">
订单查询接口的延迟在十分钟内升高数倍，随后多个下游服务出现超时。请还原这次故障的传播链路。
</Sample>

The sample is quoted as the task gives it; tasks are published in the language they were written in.

## Evaluation<About text="The submitted chain is compared node by node with the reference chain, and every model runs under the same conditions." />

<Flow>
  <Step title="What is graded">The propagation chain, in order.</Step>
  <Step title="Checks">Each node against the reference chain, and the order of the nodes.</Step>
  <Step title="Scoring">Recall of the chain's nodes; the score is the mean over 5 runs per task.</Step>
</Flow>

<Facts>
  <Fact label="Metric">Mean node recall; higher is better</Fact>
  <Fact label="Runs">5 independent runs per task</Fact>
  <Fact label="Budget">At most 100 turns per run</Fact>
  <Fact label="Same conditions">The same harnesses, turn budget and execution environment</Fact>
</Facts>
