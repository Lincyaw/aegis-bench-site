## Setting

The agent faces a service system that is failing right now, with the same observation and operation rights as the engineer on call.

<Flow>
  <Step title="The agent receives">A description of the symptoms, access to the service system, and its metrics, logs and traces.</Step>
  <Step title="How it works">It may query the observations, run operations commands and change configuration; every action is recorded.</Step>
  <Step title="What it submits">Its root cause and what it did about it. The run ends at submission.</Step>
</Flow>

<Sample task="task-008" title="支付回调超时">
支付回调在高峰时段大量超时，部分订单的状态长时间停留在「处理中」。请定位原因并恢复服务。
</Sample>

The sample is quoted as the task gives it; tasks are published in the language they were written in.

## Evaluation

Each task checks both whether the service recovered and whether the root cause is right, and every model runs under the same conditions.

<Flow>
  <Step title="What is graded">The root cause, the account of the remediation, and every action taken during the run.</Step>
  <Step title="Checks">Service health checks and a root-cause judgement against a published rubric.</Step>
  <Step title="Scoring">A task passes when the service recovered and the cause is right; the score is the mean pass rate over 5 runs per task.</Step>
</Flow>

<Facts>
  <Fact label="Metric">Pass rate; higher is better</Fact>
  <Fact label="Runs">5 independent runs per task</Fact>
  <Fact label="Budget">At most 100 turns per run</Fact>
  <Fact label="Same conditions">The same harnesses, turn budget and execution environment</Fact>
</Facts>
